Driving a truck in Europe: the kit you need

Last updated October 2026

Every country in Europe has its own rules on what you must carry in a truck, and the fines for getting it wrong are often paid on the spot. The good news is that one well-stocked cab covers almost every country on a typical route.

Before you go, check the latest advice on GOV.UK and with your operator, and make sure your paperwork is in order: driving licence, Driver CPC card, tachograph card, passport and the vehicle and load documents.

The rules that catch UK drivers out

Hi-vis within reach. In France, Spain, Italy, Belgium and many other countries the vest must be in the cab, not in a side locker, so you can put it on before you get out.

France. Vehicles over 3.5 tonnes must show 'angles morts' blind spot stickers, and you need a Crit'Air sticker to enter low-emission zones in cities such as Paris and Lyon. Order the Crit'Air sticker only from the official government site.

Winter. Austria requires trucks over 3.5 tonnes to carry snow chains from 1 November to 15 April, and mountain areas of France and Italy have their own winter equipment rules.

What to carry

  1. Tacho card and document wallet

    Keep your licence, CPC card, tacho card and passport together and dry.

    For day runs: 1 wallet
    For a week of nights out: 1 wallet

    At a roadside check you'll be asked for your driving licence, Driver CPC card, tachograph card and printouts, passport and the vehicle and load documents (such as CMR notes). A dedicated wallet keeps them together. If you drive in the EU, carry your UK GHIC health card too.

    • Slots for licence, CPC card and tacho card
    • A zipped A4 section for CMR notes and vehicle papers

  6. Headlamp beam deflectors

    Stop UK headlights dazzling drivers on the right-hand side of the road. Required by law in some countries.

    For day runs: 1 set per unit, if your headlights can't be adjusted
    For a week of nights out: 1 set per unit, if your headlights can't be adjusted

    UK headlights are angled towards the left kerb, so on the continent they shine into oncoming drivers' eyes. You need to adjust them, either with stick-on deflectors or the vehicle's own settings. Many newer trucks can switch beam pattern in the menu; check the handbook first.

  8. Crit'Air sticker (France)

    Needed to drive in low-emission zones in French cities. Buy it only from the official site. Required by law in some countries.

    For day runs: 1 per vehicle
    For a week of nights out: 1 per vehicle

    Lyon, Paris, Grenoble and other French cities have low-emission zones where trucks need a Crit'Air clean air sticker. Order it in advance, because it's posted to your address. Many websites resell the sticker for several times the price, so use the official French government site.

    • Order from certificat-air.gouv.fr, the official site
    • You'll need a scan of the vehicle registration document
    • Allow a few weeks for it to arrive

    Not sold on Amazon. Order it only from the official French government site, certificat-air.gouv.fr.

  11. Fire extinguisher

    A 1–2kg powder extinguisher can stop a small cab or tyre fire spreading to the load.

    Required in winter in parts of Europe, including Austria and the French mountains. Required by law in some countries.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a UK sticker on my truck?

You need a UK sticker unless your number plate shows the UK identifier (the letters UK, with or without the Union flag). In Spain, Cyprus and Malta you need the sticker whatever your plate shows. GB stickers are no longer valid and should be covered or removed.

Do UK trucks need French blind spot stickers?

Yes. Since 2021, all vehicles over 3.5 tonnes driving in France, including foreign ones, must show the official 'angles morts' stickers on both sides and the rear.

Do I still need a breathalyser in France?

French law still says drivers should carry one, but the fine for not having one was dropped, so it isn't enforced. Many drivers carry a twin pack anyway.

Where can I buy a Crit'Air sticker?

Only from the official French government site, certificat-air.gouv.fr. Other websites resell the same sticker for much more.
